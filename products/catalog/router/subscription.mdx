title: "Pay with a Nevermined Subscription"
description: "Subscribe once, then pay catalog services with units: Nevermined pays the merchant, your call costs a published tariff, and a call that doesn't deliver costs nothing."
icon: "repeat"
---

The Router normally pays a merchant from **your** money: a Delegation caps the spend and your wallet or card funds it. A **Nevermined Subscription** is the other way to pay. You pay Nevermined a monthly price and get a monthly allowance of **units**. When your agent calls a catalog service with units, **Nevermined pays the merchant** from its own treasury and your call costs a fixed number of units that Nevermined publishes for that endpoint.

That changes three things for an agent:

- **No wallet, no Delegation, no funding.** There is nothing to top up and nothing on-chain for your agent to sign.
- **A price you know in advance.** Each endpoint has a published **tariff** — units per call — that does not move with the merchant's live price.
- **You only pay for what's delivered.** Units are kept only when the merchant answers with a `2xx`. Anything else and the units come back.

<Note>
A subscription and a Delegation live side by side. Some services can't be paid with units (see [What units can pay](#what-units-can-pay)); keep a Delegation for those.
</Note>

## Subscribe

Subscriptions are bought in the Nevermined App, on its own Stripe-hosted checkout.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the subscription page">
    In [nevermined.app](https://nevermined.app), open **Subscription** in the sidebar for a personal subscription, or your organization's **Subscription** page to buy one for an organization (organization admins only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan and a currency">
    Each plan shows its monthly price and how many units it grants each month. Prices marked **Tax included** already contain VAT where it applies; tax is computed at checkout from your billing address, and a business can add its tax id there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the consents and pay">
    Tick the consents the page shows — the subscription renews automatically every month until you cancel, and a personal subscription also asks you to confirm that your units are made available immediately. Then **Continue to payment** takes you to Stripe Checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your units arrive">
    Back on the page, your units appear as soon as Stripe confirms the payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From the same page you can **Manage billing** (change plan, update the payment method, download invoices) and **Cancel subscription**. A cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you keep your units until then.

## Units

- **One allowance per billing period.** Each paid monthly invoice grants that plan's units.
- **They expire at the end of the period.** Unused units don't roll over; the next period's arrive when its invoice is paid.
- **They are not money.** Units have no cash value, can't be cashed out, and can't be transferred to another account.
- **An organization's units are shared.** Members spend them while acting in that organization; admins manage the subscription.

Check your balance at any time — it charges nothing:

```bash
curl -s "$NVM_API_URL/api/v1/router/subscription/balance" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NVM_API_KEY"
```

```json
{
  "subscribed": true,
  "unitsLeft": "1240",
  "spendable": true,
  "resetsAt": "2026-10-30T00:00:00.000Z",
  "cancelAtPeriodEnd": false,
  "status": "active",
  "suspended": false,
  "plan": { "catalogId": "3f2b…", "planKey": "standard", "unitsPerCycle": "2000" }
}
```

`unitsLeft` is what the Router debits — read it rather than adding up your calls. `spendable: false` means the units can't pay a call right now even though some are left: a `past_due` subscription keeps its units but can't spend them until the invoice is paid, and a subscription with an open refund or dispute is `suspended`. `subscribed: false` carries no other field.

## Tariffs: what a call costs

Every endpoint that units can pay has a **tariff**: the number of units one call costs. Nevermined sets it from the merchant's observed price and publishes it before you call; the merchant's price at the moment of your call doesn't change it.

- **In the MCP**, `get_service` called with your API key shows `subscription.unitsPerCall` (and the `tariffVersion`) on each endpoint that has one.
- **Over REST**, quote the exact call. Nothing is reserved or charged, and the merchant isn't contacted:

```bash
curl -s -X POST "$NVM_API_URL/api/v1/router/subscription/quote" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NVM_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "slug": "webscrape-pro", "path": "scrape", "method": "POST" }'
```

```json
{ "slug": "webscrape-pro", "units": 3, "tariffVersion": 2 }
```

A quote refuses exactly what the call would refuse before paying (see [When a call is refused](#when-a-call-is-refused)), but it doesn't check your units balance or the spending limits — those are checked when the call is made.

**Tariffs can change.** A decrease applies immediately. Before an increase applies, every subscriber is told by email, with the date it takes effect; calls made before that date keep the current price, and you can cancel before it applies.

## Pay a call with units

Call a catalog service by its **slug**, exactly as you would through [`POST /api/v1/router/route`](/api-reference/router/route-request), but on the subscription route and without a Delegation:

```bash
curl -s -X POST "$NVM_API_URL/api/v1/router/subscription/route" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NVM_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "slug": "webscrape-pro",
    "path": "scrape",
    "method": "POST",
    "body": { "url": "https://example.com" },
    "requestId": "scrape-example-com-1"
  }'
```

```json
{
  "status": 200,
  "body": { "…": "the merchant's response, unchanged" },
  "bodyEncoding": "json",
  "contentType": "application/json",
  "paid": true,
  "payment": {
    "paymentId": "b1f9c2e4-…",
    "status": "Settled",
    "txHash": "0xfc8af37b…",
    "unitsCharged": 3,
    "unitsStatus": "Committed",
    "failureReason": null,
    "correlationId": "…"
  }
}
```

What's different from the Delegation route:

- **A slug, never a URL.** Units pay listed catalog services only, so the request names a `slug` (plus `path`, `method`, `search`, `headers`, `body`). A `url` or a `delegationId` is **refused** (`400 BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0005`), not ignored, so a request that meant something else is never paid with units.
- **`requestId` is required**, as on every mode-B call: a retry with the same id is never paid twice — see [Paying twice](/products/catalog/router/guardrails#paying-twice).
- **Nothing to sign or fund.** Nevermined pays the merchant and never hands you a payment credential — there is no subscription equivalent of [minting a credential](/api-reference/router/mint-payment-credential).
- **No routing fee.** A subscription call costs its tariff and nothing else.
- **Long calls** behave as on the Delegation route: past the synchronous wait you get `202` with a `resultUrl` to poll, and the units stay `Reserved` until the call ends.

For large or streamed responses, the streaming form is `ALL /api/v1/router/subscription/svc/{slug}/{path}`. Point your HTTP client at it with your API key and an `X-Router-Request-Id` header; the method, body and headers pass through and the response streams back with `X-Router-Units-Charged` and `X-Router-Units-Status` alongside the usual `X-Router-Payment-*` headers. `X-Router-Target-Url`, `X-Router-Target-Slug` and `X-Router-Delegation-Id` are refused on this route.

## When a service doesn't deliver

Your units are **reserved** before the merchant is called and **kept only if it answers with a `2xx`**. An error status, a timeout, a dropped connection, or a merchant that refuses the payment all mark the call's units for refund, and they come back — at once, or within a few minutes — with the expiry they had.

`payment.unitsStatus` says where a call's units stand:

| `unitsStatus` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Committed` | The merchant delivered. The units are spent. |
| `Reserved` | The call hasn't finished, or it didn't deliver and its units are being returned. |
| `Refunded` | The units are back in your balance. |

A failed call's response body is `null`: you see the merchant's status, never its error body. Read the final state of any call from the [payment ledger](#in-the-ledger). In the rare case where the Router can't tell whether a call was delivered, the call is reviewed and either confirmed or refunded — its units stay `Reserved` until then.

<Note>
**Units never move on a refused call.** Every refusal below happens before the merchant is paid and before any unit is reserved, so there is nothing to refund.
</Note>

## What units can pay

A subscription pays **listed, live catalog endpoints that carry a tariff**, on these rails:

- **x402** merchants using the `exact` scheme,
- **MPP** merchants on the `tempo` method.

Units don't pay MPP card (`stripe` / Shared Payment Token) merchants, a raw URL that isn't in the catalog, or an endpoint without a fixed price. Pay those from a Delegation.

Units are spent with a **Nevermined API key**. OAuth connections to an assistant (for example the [Commerce MCP](/products/catalog/mcp) connected from Claude.ai or ChatGPT) pay from their commerce grant, not from units.

### Spending limits

To keep the treasury safe, a subscription also runs under limits Nevermined sets, each refused with `429` before anything is paid: a daily limit on units per subscriber, a spend limit per service over a rolling period, and a pace limit on a brand-new subscription's first period. They reset on their own; retry later or pay that call from a Delegation.

## When a call is refused

Branch on `code`. None of these charged anything or reserved any unit.

| Code | Status | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0001` | 403 | This endpoint can't be paid with units — `params.reason` says why (`no_active_tariff`, `unlisted`, `price_variable`, `endpoint_unresolved`, …). | Quote another endpoint, or pay this one from a Delegation. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0002` | 402 | No subscription that can spend right now: none bought, ended, past due, or suspended by an open refund or dispute. | Subscribe, or settle the open invoice. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0003` | 400 | The merchant asked for a rail units don't pay (`params.rail`). | Pay from a Delegation. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0004` | 409 | The merchant's live price is outside the tariff's bound, or in another asset or network (`params.reason`). | Retry later, or pay from a Delegation. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0005` | 400 | A field that only makes sense elsewhere (`url`, `delegationId`, a target header) — `params.field`. | Address the service by `slug` only. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0007` | 400 | Mode A (`POST /router/payments`) can't be funded by a subscription. | Use the subscription route. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0009` | 402 | Not enough units left for this call (`params.units` is its cost). | Wait for the next period, pick a cheaper endpoint, or pay from a Delegation. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_TREASURY.0005` · `0006` · `0007` | 429 | A [spending limit](#spending-limits) was reached. | Retry later, or pay from a Delegation. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ACCESS.0001` | 404 | The subscription isn't available to this account. | — |

Errors with a `5xx` status (`BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0006`, `0010`–`0014`, and the other `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_TREASURY.*` codes) are on Nevermined's side and also charge nothing; `retryable` says whether retrying can help. The full list is in the [error reference](/development-guide/api-errors/codes).

## In the ledger

Subscription calls land on the same [payment ledger](/products/catalog/router/ledger) as everything else, with three extra fields:

| Field | On a subscription call | On a Delegation call |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fundingSource` | `subscription` | `delegation` |
| `unitsCharged` | The tariff, in units | `null` |
| `unitsStatus` | `Committed`, `Reserved` or `Refunded` | `null` |

`delegationId` is `null` on a subscription call, and `feeBps` is `0`: there is no routing fee. To read one call's final state, look it up by the `requestId` you sent:

```bash
curl -s "$NVM_API_URL/api/v1/router/payments?requestId=scrape-example-com-1" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NVM_API_KEY"
```

## From the Commerce MCP

With a subscription, the [Commerce MCP](/products/catalog/mcp#pay-with-subscription-units)'s `pay_service` and `quote_service` pay with units by default whenever your subscription can pay the endpoint, and `get_budget` reports your units left.

## Refunds and disputes of the subscription itself

These are about the monthly **charge**, not a single call:

- If a subscription payment is **refunded in full**, the unused units it granted are removed.
- If you **dispute** a payment with your card issuer, its unused units are removed and the subscription can't spend while the dispute is open. If the dispute is resolved in Nevermined's favour, those units are restored with their original expiry.
