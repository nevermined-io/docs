The Router normally pays a merchant from **your** money: a Delegation caps the spend and your wallet or card funds it. **Nevermined credits** are the other way to pay. When your agent calls a catalog service with credits, **Nevermined pays the merchant** from its own treasury and your call costs a fixed number of credits that Nevermined publishes for that endpoint.

You get credits in three ways:

- **Free credits when you sign up.** A new account that signs up with a verified email gets 2,000 credits, with no card and no subscription. See [Free credits](#free-credits).
- **Top-ups.** Turn them on and a pack of 10,000 credits that never expire is charged to your card whenever your credits run out, up to a monthly limit you set — with or without a subscription. See [Top-ups](#top-ups).
- **A Nevermined Subscription.** You pay Nevermined a monthly price and get a monthly allowance of credits. See [Subscribe](#subscribe).

All your credits are [one balance](#credits), and they're spent the same way whatever their source. That changes three things for an agent:

- **No wallet, no Delegation, no funding.** There's no wallet to fund and nothing on-chain for your agent to sign.
- **A price you see before you call.** Each endpoint has a **rate** on Nevermined's rate card — credits per call — and the rate shown when you call is what the call costs, whatever the merchant charges at that moment (up to the rate's bound).
- **You only pay for what's delivered.** Credits are kept only when the merchant answers with a `2xx`. Anything else and the credits come back.

<Note>
**Nevermined credits are not plan credits.** Elsewhere in these docs, *credits* are what a seller's [credits-based plan](/integrate/patterns/payment-models#credits-based-plans) grants for that seller's own agent, and a *subscription* is a seller's [time-based plan](/integrate/patterns/payment-models#time-based-plans). Nevermined credits and the Nevermined Subscription are neither: they're Nevermined's own, for **buyers**, and the credits are spent only through the Router, on catalog services. On this page, "credits" always means Nevermined credits.
</Note>

Credits and a Delegation live side by side. Some services can't be paid with credits (see [What credits can pay](#what-credits-can-pay)); keep a Delegation for those.

## Free credits

A new Nevermined account that signs up with a verified email gets **2,000 free credits**, once, to try the catalog. You don't need a subscription or a card to receive them or to spend them.

- **Who gets them.** A new account that signs up with a verified email address: an email code, or a Google account. Accounts that existed before free credits launched don't get them, and neither do wallet-only sign-ups or accounts created for you by an organization, an embedded widget or an SDK.
- **Once.** They're granted once per account and once per email address in each environment. Addresses that differ only in letter case, in a `+` suffix or, for Gmail, in dots count as the same address.
- **Nothing to claim.** They're added automatically when you first sign in — or at a later sign-in, while the offer lasts — and an email confirms how many you got and when they expire.
- **Live and Sandbox.** Each environment grants its own credits. Sandbox credits pay the Sandbox test services, and Live credits pay live services.
- **They expire 30 days after they're added.** Unused free credits are lost when they expire. They aren't refunded or replaced, and credits returned for an undelivered call aren't restored if they've expired by then. A reminder email goes out 5 days before they expire, if you still have some.
- **One balance.** If you also subscribe, your free credits and your monthly allowance are one balance, spent earliest expiry first. See [Credits](#credits).

Free credits are a promotional offer, available while the offer lasts. Nevermined may remove unused free credits obtained or used in breach of its terms, for example by opening more than one account. To check whether the offer is open, and what it grants, read the public offer. It needs no API key:

```bash
curl -s "$NVM_API_URL/api/v1/credits/welcome/offer"
```

```json
{ "offer": { "credits": "2000", "ttlDays": 30 } }
```

`offer` is `null` while the offer is closed. See the [API reference](/api-reference/router/welcome-credits-offer).

## Top-ups

Top-ups keep your agent going when your credits run out. Turn them on, and each time a call needs more credits than you can spend, Nevermined charges your card for a **pack of 10,000 credits that never expire** and the call goes ahead, up to a monthly limit you set. They're for every personal account, with or without a subscription.

- **A pack is USD 10 plus applicable taxes** for 10,000 credits. The tax is calculated from your billing address, and the total is shown before you pay.
- **Top-up credits never expire.** They join your [one balance](#credits). The credits that expire soonest are spent first, so top-up credits are spent last, after your free credits and your subscription's allowance.
- **Off by default.** You turn top-ups on by buying your first pack, and the card you pay it with pays the packs after it.
- **Only when a call needs it.** While top-ups are on, a pack is charged automatically when a call costs more credits than you can spend, then the call is paid from the new credits. Nothing else charges a pack, and a call charges at most one. Any call that spends your credits can charge one: one made with your personal API key, or by an assistant you [allowed to spend your credits](#who-can-spend-your-credits) through OAuth.
- **Up to your monthly limit.** You choose a monthly limit in whole US dollars, before tax; there's no default. A pack is charged only while the packs bought this calendar month (UTC), that one included, stay within it. The lowest limit is the price of one pack, and the highest is USD 50, Nevermined's maximum.
- **Not withdrawable.** When you turn top-ups on, you ask for each pack's credits to be made available immediately and give up the 14-day right of withdrawal, for your first pack and every pack charged automatically after it. See [Refunds and disputes of a pack](#refunds-and-disputes-of-a-pack).
- **Live and Sandbox.** Each environment has its own top-ups, packs and limit. A Sandbox pack is paid in test mode, and its credits pay the Sandbox test services.

<Note>
These are **Nevermined credits** bought from Nevermined, spent only through the Router. They're not the [automatic credit top-ups](/integrate/patterns/top-up) of a seller's plan, which a Delegation pays when a request settles.
</Note>

### Turn on top-ups

You turn top-ups on in the [Nevermined App](https://nevermined.app), on the same page as your credits.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Top-ups panel">
    Open **Catalog Subscription** in the sidebar — it's in the **Buy** view — or go straight to [nevermined.app/subscription](https://nevermined.app/subscription). The catalog's **Get started** page links there too, with **Set up top-ups**. In the **Top-ups** panel, click **Turn on top-ups**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your monthly limit">
    Type a monthly limit in US dollars, before tax. The panel says how many packs a month it allows: a USD 30 limit allows up to 3 packs a month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the consents">
    Tick both boxes. The first agrees that Nevermined charges your card USD 10 plus applicable taxes for each pack of 10,000 credits that never expire: the first pack now, and another automatically each time your credits run out, up to your monthly limit. The second asks for each pack's credits to be made available immediately and acknowledges that you lose your 14-day right of withdrawal once they are, for your first pack and every pack charged automatically after it. **Top-up terms** below the boxes has the full terms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay for your first pack">
    Choose a saved card, or **Add a card** and enter its details. Confirm your billing country — with a postal code in the US and Canada — and the panel shows your total, such as **Total today: $10.81, including $0.81 tax**. Click **Pay with •••• 4242**. Your bank may ask you to confirm the payment (3-D Secure).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your credits arrive">
    Once the payment is confirmed, top-ups are on, the pack's 10,000 credits are in your balance, and an email sends you the receipt. The same card pays the packs charged automatically after this one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Manage top-ups

The **Top-ups** panel shows what you bought this month against your limit, such as **$10 of $30 this month**, and the card that pays the packs. From there:

- **Change limit** sets a new monthly limit. A limit below what you've already bought this month stops packs for the rest of the month.
- **Change card** chooses another of your cards to pay the packs.
- **Turn off top-ups** stops further packs. The credits you've bought stay in your balance. To turn top-ups on again, buy a pack again.
- **Packs** lists your packs, newest first: when each was bought (**At checkout** or **Automatic**), its credits, price and tax, its status (**Being charged**, **Paid**, **Declined**, **Not charged** or **Refunded**), and a link to its receipt.

When this month's packs reach your limit, the panel says so: no more packs are charged until the first day of next month (UTC), unless you raise the limit. Signed in, the catalog's account menu also shows where your top-ups stand, such as **Top-ups on, $10 of $50 this month**.

Nevermined emails you a receipt for every pack, a notice when this month's packs reach 50%, 75%, 90% and 100% of your limit, and a notice when an automatic pack can't be charged and top-ups pause.

### When top-ups pause

An automatic pack is charged while you're away, so a card that can't pay it **pauses** top-ups instead of retrying. While they're paused, no pack is charged, and a call that needs more credits than you have is refused (`402 BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0009`, with `params.topUps: "paused"`). The panel says why:

| The panel says | What happened | To resume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *your card declined the last automatic pack* | The card was declined. | **Buy a pack to resume**: pay for one pack with a card, as when you turned top-ups on. |
| *your bank asked to confirm the last automatic pack* | The bank asked for 3-D Secure, and nobody was there to confirm it. | **Buy a pack to resume**. |
| *the card that pays them can no longer be used* | The card was removed, or can no longer pay. | **Change card**, or **Buy a pack to resume**. |
| *Top-ups are stopped and your credits are on hold* | A card dispute is open, or was decided in the cardholder's favour. | See [Refunds and disputes of a pack](#refunds-and-disputes-of-a-pack). |

A declined pack charges nothing, and appears in **Packs** as **Declined** or **Not charged**.

### Refunds and disputes of a pack

Top-up packs can't be withdrawn: when you turned top-ups on, you asked for each pack's credits to be made available immediately and gave up the 14-day right of withdrawal, for every pack. Their credits stay in your balance and never expire. For a refund, contact Nevermined support. When a pack is refunded, its unused credits are removed from your balance, and the pack reads **Refunded** in **Packs**. A refunded pack still counts toward that month's limit.

If you **dispute** a pack with your card issuer, its unused credits are removed, none of your credits — free, top-up or subscription — can be spent, and top-ups stop while the dispute is open. If it's decided in Nevermined's favour, the pack's credits are restored, and top-ups resume once no other dispute holds them. If it's decided in your favour, your credits stay unable to spend.

## Subscribe

You buy the subscription in the [Nevermined Catalog](https://nevermined.app/catalog/), on the subscription page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the subscription page">
    In the catalog, click **Subscription** in the header, or **Subscribe for $20 a month** on the catalog's home page. Log in with your Nevermined account if the page asks you to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the subscription">
    The page compares the two ways to pay for the catalog: **Pay per call** from a budget you set up yourself, or the **Nevermined Subscription**. The subscription is **USD 20 per month plus applicable taxes**, and it grants **20,000 credits** each month. Click **Choose Subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the consents">
    Tick both consents. The first agrees that the subscription renews automatically every month until you cancel. The second asks for your credits to be made available immediately, and acknowledges that you lose the 14-day right of withdrawal once they are. Then click **Continue to payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay on the same page">
    Stripe's secure payment form opens in the page. Accept the Nevermined Subscription terms in the form, then pay. VAT or sales tax is calculated from your billing address and added on top, and the total is shown before you pay; a business can add its tax ID. Your bank may ask you to confirm this first payment (3-D Secure).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your credits arrive">
    After you pay, you land on your subscription page in the Nevermined App. Your credits appear there as soon as Stripe confirms the payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Personal accounts only

The Nevermined Subscription, top-ups and Nevermined credits belong to a **personal account**: an organization can't subscribe or buy top-ups.

- **Spend credits with a personal API key** — one with no organization tag — and without an `X-Current-Org-Id` header. A call made in an organization you're an active member of (that header, or that organization's API key) is refused with `402 BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0002`, and it **never** falls back to your personal credits.
- **Sandbox and Live are billed separately.** Each workspace has its own subscription and its own credits, and the subscription page shows which workspace you're subscribing for. A Sandbox subscription is paid in Stripe's test mode, and its credits pay the testnet services listed in Sandbox — on the catalog's services page, under **Sandbox test services**.

## Manage your subscription

In the [Nevermined App](https://nevermined.app), open **Catalog Subscription** in the sidebar — it's in the **Buy** view, so switch to it first if the App opens on Sell — or go straight to [nevermined.app/subscription](https://nevermined.app/subscription). (If you already subscribe, the catalog's subscription page takes you there too.) The page shows:

- **Credits left this month**, your plan and monthly allowance, and the date your allowance resets.
- **Credits**, when you also hold free or top-up credits: how many you can spend now, and when each part of them expires.
- **Top-ups**, where you turn them on and manage them. See [Manage top-ups](#manage-top-ups).
- **Manage billing**, which opens Stripe's customer portal, where you can update your payment method.
- **Cancel subscription**, which opens Stripe's cancellation step. A cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you can use the rest of this month's credits until then.
- **Invoices**, with each invoice's status. An invoice that was refunded reads **Refunded** or **Partially refunded**; if its refund state can't be read, it reads **Status unavailable**. A refund can take 5–10 business days to appear on your card statement.

**What your credits paid for is in Purchases.** In the Nevermined App's **Purchases** page, a call paid with credits shows **Subscription** under *Paid with* and its cost in credits (struck through when the credits came back). Filter by **Subscription credits** to see only those, and the **Credits used · this cycle** tile adds up the current billing period.

## Credits

All the credits your account holds are **one balance**, whether they're free credits, top-up credits or your subscription's monthly allowance. A call costs its rate whatever the source, and **the credits that expire soonest are spent first**.

- **One allowance per billing period.** Your plan includes a monthly allowance of 20,000 credits, available once each monthly invoice is paid. Changes to the price or the monthly allowance are announced in advance, and you can cancel before they apply.
- **The allowance expires at the end of the period.** Unused allowance credits don't roll over; your allowance resets when the next period's invoice is paid. Free credits expire 30 days after they're added, whatever your billing period.
- **Top-up credits never expire,** so they're spent last.
- **An overdue renewal freezes only the allowance.** While a subscription payment is overdue, its allowance credits can't be spent until the payment goes through. Your free and top-up credits still can, and top-ups stay on. But a call that the frozen allowance would have covered is refused (`0016`) instead of charging a pack: paying the overdue invoice is what unlocks it.
- **A card dispute freezes everything.** While a card payment you made to Nevermined — for the subscription or for a top-up pack — is disputed with your card issuer, none of your credits can be spent, and top-ups stop.
- **They are not money.** Credits have no cash value, can't be cashed out, and can't be transferred to another account.

Check your credits at any time, subscribed or not — it charges nothing:

```bash
curl -s "$NVM_API_URL/api/v1/credits/balance" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NVM_API_KEY"
```

```json
{
  "total": "11500",
  "spendable": "11500",
  "parts": [
    { "kind": "welcome", "credits": "1500", "expiresAt": "2026-11-26T09:12:44.000Z", "frozen": null },
    { "kind": "topup", "credits": "10000", "expiresAt": null, "frozen": null }
  ],
  "nextExpiry": { "credits": "1500", "at": "2026-11-26T09:12:44.000Z" },
  "topups": {
    "enabled": true,
    "monthlyLimitUsd": 30,
    "maxMonthlyLimitUsd": 50,
    "boughtThisMonthUsd": 10,
    "packPriceUsd": 10,
    "paused": null,
    "card": { "brand": "visa", "last4": "4242" }
  }
}
```

- `spendable` is what a personal API key can pay with right now — read it rather than adding up your calls. `total` also counts frozen credits.
- `parts` lists what's left by `kind` (`welcome` for free credits, `allowance` for a subscription's monthly allowance, `sla` for credits Nevermined added for a defective result, `topup` for top-up credits), in the order they're spent: earliest expiry first, never-expiring last. `expiresAt` is `null` on credits that never expire.
- `frozen` is `null` on credits you can spend, `renewal_overdue` on an allowance frozen by an overdue renewal, and `dispute` on every part while a card dispute is open.
- `nextExpiry` is the next credits to expire, or `null` when none of them expire.
- `topups` holds your [top-up](#top-ups) settings, when top-ups are available to you. It's left out when they're not, or when they couldn't be read this time.

In `topups`, amounts are US dollars before tax:

- `enabled` is whether a pack is charged automatically when your credits run out. It's `false` until you buy your first pack, and after you turn top-ups off.
- `monthlyLimitUsd` is your monthly limit, or `null` if you never set one. It's kept when you turn top-ups off.
- `maxMonthlyLimitUsd` is the highest limit Nevermined allows.
- `boughtThisMonthUsd` is what the packs bought this calendar month (UTC) cost — the figure your limit counts, refunded packs and a pack still being charged included.
- `packPriceUsd` is the price of one pack, or `null` when no pack is on sale.
- `paused` is `null`, or why no pack is being charged and since when: `{ "reason": "payment_failed", "since": "2026-10-19T10:00:00.000Z" }`. `reason` is `payment_failed` (the last automatic pack was declined), `action_required` (its card asked for authentication), `card_unavailable` (the card that pays packs can no longer pay) or `dispute` (a card dispute freezes every credit). See [When top-ups pause](#when-top-ups-pause).
- `card` is the card that pays packs (`brand` and `last4`), or `null` when none is saved.

You manage top-ups in the Nevermined App, not over the API: see [Manage top-ups](#manage-top-ups).

A caller with no credits gets `"total": "0"` and an empty `parts`. The balance is always your personal one: an organization tag on the key or an `X-Current-Org-Id` header is ignored. A call made in an organization never spends these credits, though (`402 BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0002`). See the [API reference](/api-reference/router/credits-balance).

For your subscription itself — status, plan and reset date — read the subscription balance:

```bash
curl -s "$NVM_API_URL/api/v1/router/subscription/balance" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NVM_API_KEY"
```

```json
{
  "subscribed": true,
  "creditsLeft": "1240",
  "spendable": true,
  "resetsAt": "2026-10-30T00:00:00.000Z",
  "cancelAtPeriodEnd": false,
  "status": "active",
  "suspended": false,
  "plan": { "catalogId": "3f2b…", "planKey": "standard", "creditsPerCycle": "20000" }
}
```

`creditsLeft` counts your whole balance, free credits included, frozen or not. `spendable` is about the subscription only: `true` while it's `active` or `trialing` and not `suspended` by a card dispute on its payments, `false` otherwise. An overdue subscription (`past_due` or `unpaid`) freezes only its allowance, so your free and top-up credits can still pay a call then. And a dispute on another payment you made to Nevermined freezes every credit while this still reads `true`. Read `spendable` on [`GET /api/v1/credits/balance`](#credits) for what can pay right now. `subscribed: false` carries no other field. Called in an organization you're a member of, it reads that organization's subscription, never yours; organizations can no longer subscribe, so that's normally `subscribed: false`.

### In the catalog

Signed in, the catalog shows your credits in the account menu under **Credits**, subscribed or not, and where your top-ups stand. A subscriber also sees a status bar on the catalog's home page, such as **Subscribed · 19,835 credits left this month**.

When your credits can pay a call, the catalog shows service prices **in credits** instead of USD:

- a service card priced like **15 credits**, or **from 15 credits** when its endpoints differ;
- on a service's page, the credits one call costs and how many credits you have left, with each endpoint priced in credits — or marked **Budget only** when credits can't pay it (**Own crypto wallet only** on a service that sells only to the payer's own crypto wallet);
- a switch on the services page, **Pay with your budget instead**, that turns the prices back into USD.

Everyone else sees USD prices; quote a call (below) for its price in credits.

## The rate card: what a call costs

Every endpoint that credits can pay has a **rate**: the number of credits one call costs. The rate shown when you make the call is what that call costs — the merchant's price at that moment doesn't change it. (The API calls a rate's version `tariffVersion`.)

- **In the MCP**, `get_service` called with your API key shows `subscription.creditsPerCall` (and the `tariffVersion`) on each endpoint that credits can pay.
- **Over REST**, quote the exact call. Nothing is reserved or charged, and the merchant isn't contacted:

```bash
curl -s -X POST "$NVM_API_URL/api/v1/router/subscription/quote" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NVM_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "slug": "webscrape-pro", "path": "scrape", "method": "POST" }'
```

```json
{ "slug": "webscrape-pro", "credits": 3, "tariffVersion": 2 }
```

A quote refuses what the call would refuse before contacting the merchant (see [When a call is refused](#when-a-call-is-refused)). It never checks the merchant's live price or Nevermined's treasury, and for a subscriber in good standing it doesn't check how many credits are left — those are checked when the call is made. Without a subscription in good standing, it refuses a call your spendable credits can't cover (`0002`, or `0016` when only a frozen allowance would), unless an automatic top-up would cover it.

### How a rate is set

Nevermined prices each endpoint from the merchant's own price. It takes what the merchant charges for a call, applies a markup, converts the result to credits at a nominal value per credit, and rounds **up** to the next step of a fixed scale of credit amounts — so every rate is a whole number of credits on that scale. Nevermined re-prices endpoints regularly as merchant prices move; between two pricings, the stored rate is what every call costs.

Each rate also bounds what Nevermined will pay the merchant. If the merchant's live price rises above that bound, the call is refused before anything is paid (`BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0004`), and the endpoint is flagged for re-pricing.

A subscription call carries **no Router fee**: the rate is the whole cost.

**The rate card is live.** It covers every catalog endpoint that credits can pay, and those services' own prices move all the time, so rates — and the markup, credit value and scale behind them — change, up or down, as soon as Nevermined updates them, without notice. Quote before you call when the cost matters; the rate in force at the time of the call is what you pay.

## Pay a call with credits

Call a catalog service by its **slug**, exactly as you would through [`POST /api/v1/router/route`](/api-reference/router/route-request), but on the subscription route and without a Delegation. Despite its name, the route spends any of your credits, with or without a subscription:

```bash
curl -s -X POST "$NVM_API_URL/api/v1/router/subscription/route" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NVM_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "slug": "webscrape-pro",
    "path": "scrape",
    "method": "POST",
    "body": { "url": "https://example.com" },
    "requestId": "scrape-example-com-1"
  }'
```

```json
{
  "status": 200,
  "body": { "…": "the merchant's response, unchanged" },
  "bodyEncoding": "json",
  "contentType": "application/json",
  "paid": true,
  "payment": {
    "paymentId": "b1f9c2e4-…",
    "status": "Settled",
    "txHash": "0xfc8af37b…",
    "creditsCharged": 3,
    "creditsStatus": "Committed",
    "failureReason": null,
    "correlationId": "…"
  }
}
```

What's different from the Delegation route:

- **A slug, never a URL.** Credits pay listed catalog services only, so the request names a `slug` (plus `path`, `method`, `search`, `headers`, `body`). A `url` or a `delegationId` is **refused** (`400 BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0005`), not ignored, so a request that meant something else is never paid with credits.
- **`requestId` is required**, as on every mode-B call: a retry with the same id is never paid twice — see [Paying twice](/products/catalog/router/guardrails#paying-twice).
- **Nothing to sign or fund.** Nevermined pays the merchant and never hands you a payment credential — there is no subscription equivalent of [minting a credential](/api-reference/router/mint-payment-credential).
- **No routing fee.** A subscription call costs its rate and nothing else.
- **Long calls** behave as on the Delegation route: past the synchronous wait you get `202` with a `resultUrl` to poll, and the credits stay `Reserved` until the call ends.
- **A top-up shows on the call that charged it.** When this call ran out of credits and, with [top-ups](#top-ups) on, charged a pack to cover itself, the response — the result, or the `202` — also carries `topUp`: the credits the pack added and its price before tax. Your card was charged, so tell the person you're acting for.

```json
{ "topUp": { "credits": 10000, "amountUsd": 10 } }
```

For large or streamed responses, the streaming form is `ALL /api/v1/router/subscription/svc/{slug}/{path}`. Point your HTTP client at it with your API key and an `X-Router-Request-Id` header; the method, body and headers pass through and the response streams back with `X-Router-Credits-Charged` and `X-Router-Credits-Status` alongside the usual `X-Router-Payment-*` headers, plus `X-Router-Topup-Credits` and `X-Router-Topup-Amount-Usd` when the call charged a top-up pack. `X-Router-Target-Url`, `X-Router-Target-Slug` and `X-Router-Delegation-Id` are refused on this route.

## When a service doesn't deliver

Your credits are **reserved** before the merchant is called and **kept only if it answers with a `2xx`**. An error status, a timeout, a dropped connection, or a merchant that refuses the payment all mark the call's credits for refund, and they come back automatically, with the expiry they had. If that expiry has passed by then, allowance credits come back with this period's expiry (once the subscription has ended, they're lost), and free credits are lost.

A `502` with `BCK.ROUTER.0034` (no response from the service) or `BCK.ROUTER.0025` (its reply was too large to deliver) can come after the merchant was paid. The credits are refunded all the same, but don't retry with a new `requestId` — read the outcome with the same one from the [payment ledger](#in-the-ledger).

On the streaming route, a `2xx` counts as delivered when its headers arrive: a body that breaks off mid-stream keeps its credits `Committed`. Contact support if that happens.

`payment.creditsStatus` says where a call's credits stand:

| `creditsStatus` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Committed` | The merchant delivered. The credits are spent. |
| `Reserved` | The call hasn't finished, or it didn't deliver and its credits are being returned. |
| `Refunded` | The credits were returned to your balance — except any that had expired by then, which are lost (see above). |

A failed call's response body is `null`: you see the merchant's status, never its error body.

If a service answered successfully but its result was defective, the credits are not returned automatically — contact support, and Nevermined may add credits to your current monthly allowance at its discretion (credits added this way expire at the end of the current billing period). Nevermined doesn't promise the availability or uptime of third-party services. Read the final state of any call from the [payment ledger](#in-the-ledger). In the rare case where the Router can't tell whether a call was delivered, the call is reviewed and either confirmed or refunded — its credits stay `Reserved` until then.

<Note>
**Credits never move on a refused call.** Every refusal below happens before the merchant is paid and before any credit is reserved, so there is nothing to refund.
</Note>

## What credits can pay

Credits pay **listed, live catalog endpoints that carry a rate**, on these rails:

- **x402** merchants using the `exact` scheme, paid in USDC,
- **MPP** merchants on the `tempo` method, paid in the USD stablecoin Nevermined's treasury holds there.

Credits don't pay MPP card (`stripe` / Shared Payment Token) merchants, a raw URL that isn't in the catalog, an endpoint without a fixed price, or an endpoint whose result only the paying wallet can read back — a job you poll, or something you'd own at the merchant. On a service that delivers results only to the wallet that paid, credits pay just the endpoints Nevermined has reviewed as self-contained (the paid response is the whole result); the rest are refused with `params.reason: wallet_bound`. Pay all of these from a Delegation — a `wallet_bound` endpoint only from your own crypto (`erc4337`) Delegation, since a card or organization Delegation is refused there too.

### Who can spend your credits

- **A personal Nevermined API key**: one with no organization tag, used without an `X-Current-Org-Id` header. This is how an agent you run yourself spends them, through the Catalog MCP or the API.
- **An assistant you connected through OAuth** (for example the [Catalog MCP](/mcp/catalog-mcp/overview) from Claude.ai or ChatGPT), only if you ticked **Let this assistant spend my Nevermined credits** on the consent screen. It's off by default. With it on, the assistant can read and spend your personal credits at eligible catalog services, with no separate credit cap, until you revoke the connection. With [top-ups](#top-ups) on, a call it makes that runs out of credits charges a pack to your card, like any other call. It can't start a subscription for you, turn on top-ups, or spend an organization's credits. Without it, [`pay_service`](/mcp/catalog-mcp/pay-service) pays from the connection's stablecoin spending cap instead, and a call to the credits route is refused with `403 BCK.OAUTH.0037`. An existing connection never gains this permission on its own: reconnect to allow it.

Any other kind of OAuth credential is refused with `403 BCK.OAUTH.0030`. An OAuth connection can't read [`GET /api/v1/credits/balance`](#credits) either, even one allowed to spend your credits: that balance takes a personal API key.

### No spending limits beyond your credits

Credits have no spending limit beyond your balance — no daily, per-service or pace cap. The Router's usual per-service rate limit and concurrency limit still apply (`429`). A call can also be refused when Nevermined's treasury can't pay right now — most often because its float is low (`503 BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_TREASURY.0003`, retryable). Nothing is charged; retry later.

## When a call is refused

Branch on `code`. None of these charged anything or reserved any credit.

| Code | Status | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0001` | 403 | This endpoint can't be paid with credits — `params.reason` says why (`no_active_tariff` — no rate yet, `price_variable`, `unhealthy`, `wallet_bound`, `endpoint_unresolved`, …). | Quote another endpoint, or pay this one from a Delegation — for `wallet_bound`, your own crypto (`erc4337`) Delegation. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0002` | 402 | Nothing to pay this call with: no subscription in good standing (none bought, ended or past due), and no credits — or not enough for this call — that can be spent, with no top-up that would cover it. Also the answer when you call in an organization you're a member of. | Subscribe, turn on [top-ups](#top-ups), settle the open invoice, pick a cheaper endpoint, pay from a Delegation, or call with a personal API key and no `X-Current-Org-Id`. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0003` | 400 | The merchant asked for a rail credits don't pay (`params.rail`). | Pay from a Delegation. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0004` | 409 | The merchant's live price is outside the rate's bound, or in another asset or network — or, for a service paid over an MPP **session**, it asked to be paid at an address Nevermined has not yet seen it use for this endpoint (`payee_unobserved`). `params.reason` says which. | Retry later, or pay from a Delegation. A `payee_unobserved` call stays refused until Nevermined has seen that address on this endpoint, so pay it from a Delegation. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0005` | 400 | A field that only makes sense elsewhere (`url`, `delegationId`, a target header) — `params.field`. | Address the service by `slug` only. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0007` | 400 | Mode A (`POST /router/payments`) can't be funded by a subscription — you sent it `fundingSource: "subscription"` or a `subscriptionId`. | Use the subscription route. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0009` | 402 | Not enough credits left for this call (`params.credits` is its cost). Mostly what a subscriber in good standing, or an account with top-ups on, gets: otherwise the quote usually refuses a call your credits can't cover with `0002`. With top-ups on, `params.topUps` says why no pack covered the call — see [the table below](#when-no-top-up-covers-the-call). | Pick a cheaper endpoint, turn on [top-ups](#top-ups), or pay from a Delegation. A subscriber's allowance comes back next period. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0015` | 402 | All your credits are frozen: a card payment you made to Nevermined is disputed, or a dispute was decided in the cardholder's favour. | Withdraw the dispute with your card issuer, or contact Nevermined support. Until then, pay from a Delegation. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0016` | 402 | Your subscription's renewal payment is overdue, so its allowance is frozen, and your other credits don't cover this call (`params.credits` is its cost). | Update your card on the subscription page so the invoice is paid, or pay from a Delegation. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_TREASURY.0002` | 422 | The merchant wants to be paid in an asset Nevermined's treasury doesn't pay in. | Pay from a Delegation. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_TREASURY.0003` | 503 | Nevermined's treasury float can't cover this payment right now. | Retry later (`retryable: true`). |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_TREASURY.0008` | 403 | Nevermined's treasury can't pay this merchant. | Pay another service. |
| `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ACCESS.0001` | 404 | The subscription isn't available on this deployment or to this account. | — |
| `BCK.CATALOG.0001` | 404 | No listed, payable catalog service has that `slug`. | Check the slug in the catalog. |
| `BCK.OAUTH.0030` | 403 | An OAuth credential that isn't a commerce connection. | Use your personal API key. |
| `BCK.OAUTH.0037` | 403 | An OAuth commerce connection you didn't allow to spend your credits. | Reconnect, and tick **Let this assistant spend my Nevermined credits** on the consent screen. |
| `BCK.OAUTH.0038` | 403 | An OAuth connection allowed to spend your credits, used in an organization. It spends only your personal credits. | Call without the organization context. |
| `BCK.ROUTER.0001` | 400 | With the message *this service delivers its results only to the personal wallet that paid*: the Router's own check that a wallet-bound endpoint is one credits can pay (such calls are normally refused earlier, as `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0001` `wallet_bound`). Any other message is a malformed request. | Pay it from your own crypto (`erc4337`) Delegation. |

### When no top-up covers the call

With top-ups on, a call that runs out of credits charges a pack and goes ahead. When it can't, it's refused with `402 BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0009`, and `params.topUps` says why. No credits were spent on the call in any case.

| `params.topUps` | What happened | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `paused` | A pack was declined or needed authentication, now or earlier, so top-ups are paused. | Resume them from the Top-ups panel — see [When top-ups pause](#when-top-ups-pause). |
| `limitReached` | One more pack would take this month past your monthly limit, or past Nevermined's maximum. | Raise your limit in the Top-ups panel, or wait for next month (UTC). |
| `pending` | A pack is being charged and wasn't confirmed in time. Your card may have been charged; its credits land once the payment is recorded. | Retry in a moment. |
| `unavailable` | No pack could be charged right now, and none was. | Retry later. |
| `bought` | This call charged a pack, but other calls spent its credits first. The credits are yours. | Retry. |

A `0009` without `params.topUps` tried no pack: top-ups are off, or not available to you. A call that a frozen allowance would have covered (`0016`) or that a card dispute froze (`0015`) never charges a pack.

Other errors with a `5xx` status (`BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTER.0006` and `0010`–`0014`, and the remaining `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_TREASURY.*` codes) are on Nevermined's side and also charge nothing; `retryable` says whether retrying can help. The Router's common errors apply too — a reused `requestId` (`409 BCK.ROUTER.0002`), the rate and concurrency limits (`429`): see [every error code](/products/catalog/router/guardrails#every-error-code).

A service paid over an MPP **session** (a payment channel) can also answer `409 BCK.ROUTER.0042` or `503 BCK.ROUTER.0043`. Nevermined pays it over **one channel shared by everyone paying with credits**, so while that channel is busy, or closing — when its deposit reaches its ceiling, or when the service refuses the channel or a top-up of it; the next call after the close opens a new one — calls to that service are refused for everyone. Closing usually takes about a minute, and 15 minutes or more when the service does not accept a cooperative close. Nothing is charged; retry later with the same `requestId`. The full list is in the [error reference](/development-guide/api-errors/codes). `BCK.SUBSCRIPTION_TREASURY.0004`–`0007` and `0014` are retired: they belonged to spending limits removed before launch, and the API no longer returns them.

## In the ledger

Calls paid with credits land on the same [payment ledger](/products/catalog/router/ledger) as everything else, with three extra fields. `fundingSource` is `subscription` on every credits call, free credits included:

| Field | On a credits call | On a Delegation call |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fundingSource` | `subscription` | `delegation` |
| `creditsCharged` | The call's rate, in credits — kept when the credits are refunded, so count it only when `creditsStatus` is `Committed` | `null` |
| `creditsStatus` | `Committed`, `Reserved` or `Refunded` | `null` |

`delegationId` is `null` on a credits call, and `feeBps` is `0`: there is no routing fee. To read one call's final state, look it up by the `requestId` you sent:

```bash
curl -s -G "$NVM_API_URL/api/v1/router/payments" \
  --data-urlencode "requestId=scrape-example-com-1" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NVM_API_KEY"
```

```json
{
  "presence": "present",
  "payments": [
    { "paymentId": "b1f9c2e4-…", "requestId": "scrape-example-com-1", "fundingSource": "subscription", "creditsCharged": 3, "creditsStatus": "Committed", "delegationId": null, "feeBps": 0 }
  ]
}
```

`presence: "absent"` means no payment is recorded for that `requestId` yet — while the call may still be running, retry it with the same `requestId`, never a new one. See [Look up one payment by `requestId`](/products/catalog/router/ledger#look-up-one-payment-by-requestid).

## From the Catalog MCP

With credits and a personal API key — or an OAuth connection [allowed to spend them](#who-can-spend-your-credits) — the [Catalog MCP](/mcp/catalog-mcp/overview)'s [`pay_service`](/mcp/catalog-mcp/pay-service#pay-with-subscription-credits) pays, and [`quote_service`](/mcp/catalog-mcp/quote-service#quote-in-subscription-credits) prices, in credits by default when your credits and the endpoint qualify. With a personal API key, [`get_budget`](/mcp/catalog-mcp/get-budget#nevermined-credits) and [`wallet_balance`](/mcp/catalog-mcp/wallet-balance#nevermined-credits) report your credits. When a call charges a top-up pack, `pay_service` says so in [`topUpMessage`](/mcp/catalog-mcp/pay-service#when-a-call-charges-a-top-up-pack), for your assistant to tell you.

## Refunds and disputes of the subscription itself

These are about the monthly **fee**, not a single call. Subscription fees are **non-refundable**, except where the law requires a refund or for billing errors.

- If a subscription payment is **refunded in full**, the unused credits it granted are removed. A refund can take 5–10 business days to appear on your card statement.
- If you **dispute** a payment with your card issuer, its unused credits are removed and none of your credits — free and top-up credits included — can be spent while the dispute is open, so no top-up pack is charged either. If the dispute is decided in your favour, they stay unable to spend. If it's decided in Nevermined's favour, those credits are restored with their original expiry — credits whose billing period has ended by then come back already expired.
