## Before you connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="A Nevermined account">
    Free, at [nevermined.app](https://nevermined.app). You never share an API key with HOST_LABEL — the consent cap *is* the credential.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Something to spend from">
    A paid call draws on a budget you control. You can set one up two ways:
    - **A spending cap you approve during connect** — the default. Nothing to prepare in advance: you approve it in [step 3](#approve-the-spending-cap), funded from your own wallet (crypto) or a card. This is a [commerce grant](/mcp/catalog-mcp/overview#auth-&-transport) — a capped, revocable budget, not a key.
    - **A [Nevermined Subscription](/products/catalog/router/subscription)** — a monthly credit allowance Nevermined spends for you, with no wallet or Delegation to fund.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Start on **sandbox**, where nothing costs real money, before you point HOST_LABEL at **live**.
</Note>

## Approve the spending cap

{/* HOST-SPECIFIC trigger, SHARED body. Replace WHEN_CONSENT_FIRES with when the host runs the consent:
    "at the first paid call", "at creation", or "when you connect". */}

HOST_LABEL opens Nevermined's consent page WHEN_CONSENT_FIRES. It is the one place a human is required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check who and where">
    The page shows the **Sandbox / Live** badge and **who is asking**. On **live**, the cap is real money.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the cap and validity">
    Set the **spending cap** (USDC or EURC) and how long the grant is **valid for**, then **Grant access**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That approval is a **commerce grant**: it spends only from the budget you just approved, only through the [Router](/products/catalog/router/overview), and you can revoke it any time. Nevermined's routing fee comes out of the same cap, and the grant can also read your payment history and wallet balance — [the cap bounds what it can spend, not what it can see](/mcp/catalog-mcp/overview#what-the-consent-page-asks).
