Perplexity can browse the [Nevermined Catalog](/products/catalog/overview) and pay for services as it works, without you handing over an API key. You add one connector, approve a single spending cap, and Perplexity does the rest. This is the per-host walkthrough; for the concept and the other assistants, see [Using the Catalog from your web assistant](/products/catalog/from-your-web-assistant).

## Before you connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="A Nevermined account">
    Free, at [nevermined.app](https://nevermined.app). You never share an API key with Perplexity — the consent cap *is* the credential.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A funded wallet">
    A paid call draws on a **spending cap** you approve during connect — a [commerce grant](/mcp/catalog-mcp/overview#auth-&-transport): a capped, revocable budget funded from your own wallet, not a key. Fund that wallet first with the asset the service settles in — see [Fund your wallet](/products/catalog/router/quickstart#3-fund-your-wallet). You approve the cap itself in [Approve the spending cap](#approve-the-spending-cap).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Start on **sandbox**, where you use testnet funds and nothing costs real money, before you point Perplexity at **live**.
</Note>

<Note>
Custom remote connectors need a **Perplexity Pro or Enterprise** plan. On an organization, custom connectors are off by default — an admin enables **Allow members to add custom connectors** first.
</Note>

## Add the Catalog to Perplexity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the connector settings">
    In Perplexity, go to **Settings → Connectors** (individual plans may show it under **Customize → Connectors**; Enterprise admins add it in the organization's connector settings) and choose **Add custom connector**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the server URL">
    Give it a name and the URL for the environment you want:

    | Environment | Server URL |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Sandbox** — the right place for a first run | `https://mcp.sandbox.nevermined.app/mcp` |
    | **Live** — real merchants, real money | `https://mcp.live.nevermined.app/mcp` |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the OAuth client">
    Choose **OAuth 2.0** for authentication. Enter the **Client ID** `perplexity` and leave the **Client Secret blank** — Nevermined accepts public PKCE ("Native") clients only, so there is no secret. Perplexity's own callback (`https://www.perplexity.ai/rest/connections/oauth_callback`, or the `enterprise.perplexity.ai` equivalent on an Enterprise subdomain) is already registered; you don't configure a redirect URI.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Approve the spending cap

Perplexity opens Nevermined's consent page when you connect the connector. It is the one place a human is required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check who and where">
    The page shows the **Sandbox / Live** badge and **who is asking**. On **live**, the cap is real money.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the cap and validity">
    Set the **spending cap** (USDC or EURC) and how long the grant is **valid for**, then **Grant access**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That approval is a **commerce grant**: it spends only from the budget you just approved, only through the [Router](/products/catalog/router/overview), and you can revoke it any time. Nevermined's routing fee comes out of the same cap, and the grant can also read your payment history and wallet balance — [the cap bounds what it can spend, not what it can see](/mcp/catalog-mcp/overview#what-the-consent-page-asks).

## Make your first paid call

Ask for something only live data can answer, and name the connector so Perplexity reaches for it rather than answering from memory.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse for free">
    Ask *"list the Nevermined catalog categories"* — a free tool call, no sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask it to buy">
    *"Buy the service with slug `<slug>` through the Nevermined connector and show me the response."* The paid call draws on the cap you approved and returns the merchant's response.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
If Perplexity answers without paying, it answered from its own knowledge — name the connector and ask for live data. More on phrasing: [Catalog MCP tips](/mcp/catalog-mcp/overview#connect-from-your-assistant).
</Tip>

## Disconnect

- **Revoke the budget (Nevermined side).** Open [Connections](https://nevermined.app/connections) and disconnect Perplexity. The credential stops working on its next call; discovery still works.
- **Remove the connector (Perplexity side).** In **Settings → Connectors**, remove the Nevermined custom connector.

To spend again — after you disconnect, or after a cap is used up — re-run the connect step and approve a **new** cap. A budget is never widened or extended in place.
