ChatGPT can browse the [Nevermined Catalog](/products/catalog/overview) and pay for services as it works, without you handing over an API key. You add one MCP app, approve a single spending cap, and ChatGPT does the rest. This is the per-host walkthrough; for the concept and the other assistants, see [Using the Catalog from your web assistant](/products/catalog/from-your-web-assistant).

<Note>
This covers the **manual** connection — adding the Catalog as a custom MCP app in Developer mode, which works today. A one-click listing in ChatGPT's connector directory is still in progress — see [From the ChatGPT directory](#from-the-chatgpt-directory).
</Note>

## Before you connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="A Nevermined account">
    Free, at [nevermined.app](https://nevermined.app). You never share an API key with ChatGPT — the consent cap *is* the credential.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A paid ChatGPT plan">
    Developer mode, where custom MCP apps live, needs a paid plan (Plus or above).
  </Step>
  <Step title="A funded wallet">
    A paid call draws on a **spending cap** you approve during connect — a [commerce grant](/mcp/catalog-mcp/overview#auth-&-transport): a capped, revocable budget funded from your own wallet, not a key. Fund that wallet first with the asset the service settles in — see [Fund your wallet](/products/catalog/router/quickstart#3-fund-your-wallet). You approve the cap itself in [step 3](#approve-the-spending-cap).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Start on **sandbox**, where you use testnet funds and nothing costs real money, before you point ChatGPT at **live**.
</Note>

## Add the Catalog to ChatGPT

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Developer mode">
    **Settings → Security and login → Developer mode.** *Plugins* then appears in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an MCP app">
    **Plugins → ＋ → Create app → Create MCP app.** (The ＋ menu's *Create plugin* builds a plugin from scratch — not what you want.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the dialog">
    - **Name** — read the warning below first; you only get one shot at it.
    - **Connection** — leave it on **Server URL** and paste the URL for your environment:

      | Environment | Server URL |
      | --- | --- |
      | **Sandbox** — the right place for a first run | `https://mcp.sandbox.nevermined.app/mcp` |
      | **Live** — real merchants, real money | `https://mcp.live.nevermined.app/mcp` |

    - **Authentication** — **OAuth**, with the client ID and secret **empty**. ChatGPT discovers the OAuth settings from the server itself; leave **Advanced OAuth settings** closed.
    - Tick **I understand and want to continue** under the *"Custom MCP servers introduce risk"* notice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Choose the name carefully — a ChatGPT plugin cannot be deleted or renamed.** Names are unique per account, so a name you have used is taken for good, and uninstalling does not free it.
</Warning>

## Approve the spending cap

ChatGPT runs Nevermined's consent **at creation** — immediately, before any tool call. It is the one place a human is required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger the consent">
    When you create the MCP app, ChatGPT shows *Add Nevermined … to ChatGPT?* and opens the consent page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check who and where">
    The page shows the **Sandbox / Live** badge and that **chatgpt.com** is asking. On **live**, the cap is real money.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the cap and validity">
    Set the **spending cap** (USDC or EURC) and how long the grant is **valid for**, then **Grant access**. The plugin shows as connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That approval is a **commerce grant**: it spends only from the budget you just approved, only through the [Router](/products/catalog/router/overview), and you can revoke it any time. Nevermined's routing fee comes out of the same cap, and the grant can also read your payment history and wallet balance — [the cap bounds what it can spend, not what it can see](/mcp/catalog-mcp/overview#what-the-consent-page-asks).

## Make your first paid call

Ask for something only live data can answer, and name the connector so ChatGPT reaches for it rather than answering from memory.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse for free">
    In a new chat the plugin is available automatically. Ask *"list the Nevermined catalog categories"* — a free call, no sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask it to buy">
    *"Buy the service with slug `<slug>` through the Nevermined connector and show me the response."* Each paid call first asks *Allow ChatGPT to use Nevermined …?* — **Allow** (once, or always), and it returns the merchant's response.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
If ChatGPT answers without paying, it answered from its own knowledge — name the connector and ask for live data. Confirm the connection by asking for the Catalog categories, which is a free call that either works or doesn't. More on phrasing: [Catalog MCP tips](/mcp/catalog-mcp/overview#connect-from-your-assistant).
</Tip>

## Disconnect

- **Revoke the budget (Nevermined side).** Open [Connections](https://nevermined.app/connections) and disconnect ChatGPT. The credential stops working on its next call; discovery still works. On the next paid call ChatGPT shows a **Reconnect Nevermined** card (its wording says the connection "expired" even when you revoked it).
- **Remove the connector (ChatGPT side).** Uninstall the plugin from **Plugins**. Note the plugin **cannot be deleted or renamed**, and uninstalling does not free its name.

To spend again — after you disconnect, or after a cap is used up — re-run the connect step and approve a **new** cap. A budget is never widened or extended in place.
