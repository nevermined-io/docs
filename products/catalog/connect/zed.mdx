Zed can browse the [Nevermined Catalog](/products/catalog/overview) and pay for services as it works, without you handing over an API key. You add one server, approve a single spending cap, and Zed does the rest. This is the per-host walkthrough; for the concept and the other assistants, see [Using the Catalog from your web assistant](/products/catalog/from-your-web-assistant).

## Before you connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="A Nevermined account">
    Free, at [nevermined.app](https://nevermined.app). You never share an API key with Zed — the consent cap *is* the credential.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A funded wallet">
    A paid call draws on a **spending cap** you approve during connect — a [commerce grant](/mcp/catalog-mcp/overview#auth-&-transport): a capped, revocable budget funded from your own wallet, not a key. Fund that wallet first with the asset the service settles in — see [Fund your wallet](/products/catalog/router/quickstart#3-fund-your-wallet). You approve the cap itself in [Approve the spending cap](#approve-the-spending-cap).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Start on **sandbox**, where you use testnet funds and nothing costs real money, before you point Zed at **live**.
</Note>

## Add the Catalog to Zed

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the server">
    Open **Settings → AI → MCP Servers** (or run the **agent: open settings** action), click **Add Server → Add Remote Server**, and enter a name and the URL for your environment:

    | Environment | Server URL |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Sandbox** — the right place for a first run | `https://mcp.sandbox.nevermined.app/mcp` |
    | **Live** — real merchants, real money | `https://mcp.live.nevermined.app/mcp` |

    Leave the headers and OAuth Client ID fields blank — Zed runs the standard MCP OAuth flow for you (the Catalog is a public PKCE client). Zed stores it under `context_servers` in your settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    With no auth header set, Zed prompts you to authenticate — Nevermined's consent page. Watch the indicator dot next to the server name turn ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Approve the spending cap

Zed opens Nevermined's consent page when Zed prompts you to authenticate the server. It is the one place a human is required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check who and where">
    The page shows the **Sandbox / Live** badge and **who is asking**. On **live**, the cap is real money.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the cap and validity">
    Set the **spending cap** (USDC or EURC) and how long the grant is **valid for**, then **Grant access**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That approval is a **commerce grant**: it spends only from the budget you just approved, only through the [Router](/products/catalog/router/overview), and you can revoke it any time. Nevermined's routing fee comes out of the same cap, and the grant can also read your payment history and wallet balance — [the cap bounds what it can spend, not what it can see](/mcp/catalog-mcp/overview#what-the-consent-page-asks).

## Make your first paid call

Ask for something only live data can answer, and name the connector so Zed reaches for it rather than answering from memory.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse for free">
    Ask *"list the Nevermined catalog categories"* — a free tool call, no sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask it to buy">
    *"Buy the service with slug `<slug>` through the Nevermined connector and show me the response."* The paid call draws on the cap you approved and returns the merchant's response.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
If Zed answers without paying, it answered from its own knowledge — name the connector and ask for live data. More on phrasing: [Catalog MCP tips](/mcp/catalog-mcp/overview#connect-from-your-assistant).
</Tip>

## Disconnect

- **Revoke the budget (Nevermined side).** Open [Connections](https://nevermined.app/connections) and disconnect Zed. The credential stops working on its next call; discovery still works.
- **Remove the connector (Zed side).** Remove the server from **Settings → AI → MCP Servers** (or delete its `context_servers` entry in your settings).

To spend again — after you disconnect, or after a cap is used up — re-run the connect step and approve a **new** cap. A budget is never widened or extended in place.
